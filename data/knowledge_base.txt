Project Nebula:
- Project Nebula is a secret initiative by AstroCorp to build a moon base by 2030.
- The project is led by Dr. Elena Vance.
- The budget for Project Nebula is $50 billion.
- It uses a new type of propulsion system called 'Ion-Pulse'.

AstroCorp Vacation Policy:
- Employees get 25 days of paid time off per year.
- Unlimited sick leave is provided.
- Employees can work from anywhere in the solar system for 2 months a year.

Company Mascot:
- AstroCorp's mascot is a robot dog named 'Luna'.
- Luna was created in 2022.
